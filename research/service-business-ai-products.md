# AI agents in service-business workflows

Focused research for the private note to Irv. Sources were checked on August 21, 2026. Each source is an official product page or help article from the company that owns the product.

## What the products are doing

### Jobber Receptionist

- **What it handles:** Incoming calls and text messages, questions about the business, job requests, bookings, cancellations, rescheduling, appointment details, and notes from customers. When a customer is not ready to book, it can create a follow-up task for the team.
- **How it fits the existing workflow:** It uses the business's Jobber profile, request forms, online-booking rules, and calendar. Bookings, requests, conversation summaries, and follow-up tasks appear inside Jobber.
- **What remains under human control:** The business chooses which actions are enabled, which services and team members can be booked, and which words should trigger an escalation. A team member can receive a text with context or take over the live call.
- **Direct source:** [Jobber Help Center: Receptionist powered by Jobber AI](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/receptionistpowered-by-jobber-ai/)
- **Exact fact useful in the note:** If a caller is not ready to schedule, Jobber creates a follow-up task in the business's schedule with a summary of the conversation. The agent leaves the person a clear next step instead of losing the enquiry.

### Housecall Pro CSR AI

- **What it handles:** Customer calls and chats, common questions, collecting caller information, checking availability, and booking jobs.
- **How it fits the existing workflow:** It reads the business's existing Housecall Pro services, hours, availability, service area, and booking settings. New jobs go straight onto the Housecall Pro calendar, while call summaries and customer details are logged in the call log and inbox.
- **What remains under human control:** The business sets the hours, services, prices, scripts, tone, and booking rules. It also chooses the number that receives a call when the customer asks for a person.
- **Direct source:** [Housecall Pro: CSR AI](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/ai-team/csr-ai/)
- **Exact fact useful in the note:** When someone asks for a person, CSR AI gathers their information first and sends the call to a number chosen by the business. The handoff carries useful context into the human conversation.

### ServiceTitan AI Virtual Agent

- **What it handles:** Inbound calls, customer questions, gathering job details, booking, confirming appointments, and rescheduling. It also produces a summary and transcript for each call.
- **How it fits the existing workflow:** It uses live ServiceTitan customer records, addresses, job types, technician availability, and business rules. Confirmed work goes directly onto the existing dispatch board.
- **What remains under human control:** The business decides which job types the agent may book, the wording around fees, and the opening and closing messages. Calls outside the defined rules or requiring judgment can transfer to a live customer-service representative. The team can review recordings and booking outcomes.
- **Direct source:** [ServiceTitan: AI Virtual Agent](https://www.servicetitan.com/features/pro/virtual-agent)
- **Exact fact useful in the note:** ServiceTitan combines live schedule data with rules set by the business, then transfers calls that fall outside those rules to a person. This is a concrete version of the agent preparing or completing routine work while preserving human judgment.

## The pattern worth carrying into the note

Across these products, the useful unit is a customer conversation connected to the system the business already uses. The agent can collect details and complete routine schedule work. The business defines the limits, sees what happened, and receives the conversation when judgment is needed.

This supports the shape already present in the note without requiring a market overview. One or two linked sentences are enough.
